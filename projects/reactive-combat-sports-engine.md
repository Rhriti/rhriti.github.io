An audio-led performance platform designed to bring high-fidelity coaching to solo training.

**The Story :** Combat sports are the oldest and most lively form of competition, yet solo training-specifically shadowboxing-is often a static, non-reactive experience. I wanted to build a tool that replicated the "call-and-response" energy of a physical coach, accessible to anyone with a smartphone.

I started building this as a solopreneur. Since January, the platform has seen a 4,065% increase in sessions. It has been a fascinating exercise in global product-market fit; without traditional marketing, the project has reached 1.9k active users across 140 countries.

The technical challenge was ensuring the experience was "sticky" enough to compete with a physical gym. Currently, the platform facilitates over 200 hours of active usage, maintaining a 80.29% engagement rate. In high-adoption regions like India, users are engaging for an average of 13 minutes and 26 seconds per session, effectively doubling the global baseline for digital training engagement.
