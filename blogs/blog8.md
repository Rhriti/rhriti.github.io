<h2 style="color: #245ab3;">Vikram Betal and Examinations</h2>
Date-26/11/2026
<p>
Why Vikram Betal and Examination? coz they link heavily with one another. Here's the thing about the epic "Vikram Betal" - it's one of a kind story which relates to children, teens , young chuds, middle-aged, old folks and spritually inclined people as well. everyone has their own interpretation of the epic. I'll narrate the one of highest form.
</p>
<p>
Betal makes a deal with Vikram. If vikram is unfazed by his 'n' stories he'd go along with him but if he replies to him he'd go back to the banyan tree. Looks pretty simple isn't it? keep the ears closed and mouth shut. But the problem is that when you are in the moment, living the story, you become the doer and it's hard not to take action and be witness, when you identify yourself as the doer/part of the story.
</p>
<p>
How does this relate to exams? In exams you know what's about to come, no jagron, no proofs , no deep-questions, no BS and pure plain problems as per syllabus . You couldn't resist but dive deep once you start preparing. you are the vikram who find's it hard to be on track of "what's coming in paper" and do what's needed for the exam, you do the extra coz you identify as the doer.
</p>
<p>
The concepts, topic didn't matter a day ago but now it's your life. You feel like cheating (filled with guilt) when you skip a niche, not-so important topic which might never come in the paper. Such is the mind of a doer, hazy, filled with crap and can't see what's obvious and what's a pitfall.
</p>
